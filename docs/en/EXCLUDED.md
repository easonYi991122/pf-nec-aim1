# Exclusions, dependency cuts and supported scope

[fact] Export reduction does not reassess prior scientific results. Internal reports are not shipped; key numbers are in RESULTS. The final LightGBM paths do not depend on the following excluded material.

|Cut|Reason|Effect|
|---|---|---|
|Unpublished PI code, trained model, loading / checking tools and source-line audit|Sharing boundary|No content, weights, parameter extraction or source paraphrase exported|
|Model-derived XGB learner|Excluded parameter source|Only LGB is exposed; no substituted parameters presented as reproduction|
|PI-29 family, source bank and dependent menus|Model-derived material excluded|Historical results remain valid, but original selection, sequence, attribution and learning-curve fits cannot be rerun; no silent menu reduction|
|Machine paths, excluded families and orchestration text in the original spec|Outside sharing scope|Keep the already-exported frozen spec's own checksum and original-spec provenance; this revision does not change frozen logic|
|Original CORE driver and import-time directory side effects|Old-workspace dependency|Retain only pure row / split / hash functions and private-cache interfaces|
|Old dictionary mappings and data-derived crosswalks|Dictionary / derivatives not distributed|Infer locally from authorised clean/raw inputs; ambiguity fails|
|Historical job DAG, dispatcher, device / queue bridges|Not needed for local reproduction|Retain fitting-pool boundaries, stopping rounds, ledger and resource guard|
|Full historical T3 report driver|Bound to excluded models / evidence|Retain interpret, t3_fast and G-safe read-only packet methods, not full historical report reconstruction|
|I7 original curve-training driver|Excluded family dependency|Retain 8-background-variable slices, nested sampling and statistics; freeze new curves separately|
|Aim 1b historical report assembly / evidence loading|Methods-only handoff|Support measurement, timing, eligibility, positivity, stage3_cards, trial_skeleton, draft_dag and rank_cards; schema in explore/README; no bundled historical evidence or effect estimate|
|Sequence / M1 / GPU training code|Did not pass the pre-specified gate and adds historical dependencies|Retain results and stopping boundaries only; search-pause conditions in STATUS_AND_NEXT|
|Data, dictionary, caches, weights, predictions, row logs, hosts / credentials and internal agent tools|Code-sharing boundary|Patient-level outputs remain in authorised private locations|

T3 PI10 is a fixed 10-variable interpretation group in the original frozen spec, assembled from internal T007 tables; it is not read from a trained model and exposes no source-bank learner. T007 is an internal report, not shipped; its relevant use is explained here.

stage3_cards does not require a PI-29 model; it reads caller-supplied aggregate fields. Missing model evidence must remain absent or null, not be borrowed from another anatomical site; the discussion order of 13 cards does not establish causal identification. trial_skeleton and draft_dag support only the 9 questions in STAGE4_IDS. All original exclusions remain in force.
