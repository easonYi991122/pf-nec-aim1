For training terms and single-fit examples, see [synthetic usage](TEAM_USAGE.md): fit trains a model; child is the current training subset; refit trains on the outer training pool; apply uses no labels. A family is the complete prespecified comparison set; epoch counts training passes, patience is the early-stopping wait, and batch is the sample count per update. Devices and allocations for the NVIDIA parallel-computing platform (CUDA) still require confirmation and data authorization.

These cover bounded tabular and missingness-modeling tasks (T-2), early-postoperative ordering (T-3), postoperative-first-day stratification (T-4), field-availability auditing (T-5) and signal interfaces and linkage governance (T-6).

## Start here: terms used in the cards

The project aims to identify neonates at risk of necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) after heart surgery, using information already available at prediction time. “Passing” a task means research acceptance, not approval for clinical use. See [GLOSSARY](GLOSSARY.md) for other abbreviations.

Time notation: postoperative day (POD), counted from zero on surgery day.

## Shared acceptance gates

- Split history: PI72-CLEAN r1–5 and Task A / Task B r0–4 have already been seen. New seeds or extension from 3 to 5 repeats do not restore independence. Label all new results “replication / development evidence”. D1 creates no new holdout from existing data; it records only an intent to isolate future new data, with access, training and selection logs sealed before we fix use and evaluation together to preserve the independence of future evidence.

- Statistical gate: pair each difference only within the same task, risk set, label, timing and split. Require 5 complete repeats, mean paired AUROC gain at least 0.01 and a simultaneous 95% lower bound above 0 for the full declared family; at least 1990 of 2000 stratified hospitalization bootstrap draws must be jointly valid. A 3-repeat run is a screen only. Weight repeats equally and share hospitalization multiplicities across repeats and models; missing arms, single classes or inadequate support yield incomplete/null, without shrinking the family or redrawing.

- Alert research convention: after blind reviews, the common evaluation addendum is installed and frozen at v1.2 (2026-10-07). The 5 alerts per 100 at-risk patient-days, minimum 5-percentage-point detection gain and 3-day cooldown are predeclared research choices, not PI-endorsed clinical thresholds. Shared missing-day, denominator and deduplication rules make detection and burden comparable; passing a research gate is not clinical readiness, and neither AUROC 0.01 nor historical negative-day 5% false positive rate (FPR) replaces alert evaluation.

- Separate alert reports: retrospective capacity curves ranking all days, and policies with thresholds set on inner out-of-fold predictions (OOF) predictions and applied externally. Both report actual alerts per 100 at-risk days, deduplicated case detection, positive predictive value (PPV), alerts per 1 detected case, missed cases and denominators, plus paired detection differences at equal burden. Fit thresholds and calibrators without outer-test data. Sampling and outcome selection in PI72-CLEAN / formal prevent extrapolation to deployment burden.

- Timing gate: apply D5-safe masking to each historical day before constructing windows; surgery information is unavailable preoperatively and intensive care unit (ICU) / first 2-hour information uses the frozen availability gates. Resolve any T-5 timing issue affecting an intended input before using it; “unconfirmed” does not mean “leakage”. Disclose later-data-era flag with possible follow-up truncation (late_era) truncation, unresolved hospitalization-to-person linkage and the distinction between the local clean contract and the manuscript scoring design.

- Resource gate: the caps below are new planning limits, not measured timings. Count retries, preprocessing, inference, occupied queue time and inner threshold fitting; forecast total use from the first planned fit and stop as incomplete if over budget, without reducing seeds / repeats / menus. Use 2 numerical threads per process, an 8 gibibyte in binary units (GiB) resident set size (RSS) cap, a single graphics processing unit (GPU) job and a 6 GiB GPU-memory cap; identical-configuration resumption also counts.

- T-2/T-3 share a preregistered contrast ledger: 4 T-2 contrasts plus 6 T-3 contrasts form a joint simultaneous family of 10. Share hospitalization sampling weights, but calculate each difference inside its own declared row set; never subtract AUROCs across risk sets. We need to implement stratum-mask support for this new family together and arrange blind review by a partner not involved in its implementation, avoiding favourable row selection; do not relabel an old family. T-4 belongs to a separate Task B family.
